The strongest plans succeed because they are implemented in environments ready to support them. Cultural assessments and maturity models show leaders what drives or hinders progress. Compliments and Complaints

Based on Innolead document INNOLEAD-F39 (rev. 1)

Our promise

We aim to provide a quality service in an open and accountable way. We make it easy to raise a compliment or complaint, treat every complaint as feedback that deserves a prompt and polite response, apologise where we are wrong, and use what we learn to improve.

Compliments

We are glad to hear when you are satisfied. We record every compliment, acknowledge it and pass it to the relevant manager so the team receives your feedback.

What counts as a complaint

Any expression of dissatisfaction, however it is made: in person, by phone, in writing or by email. You do not need to use the word “complaint”.

How to complain

1

Raise it informally

Tell a member of our team. Many concerns are resolved quickly and informally, sometimes with a conversation between you and the person concerned.

2

Make a formal complaint

If it is not resolved, complain verbally or in writing. We acknowledge it in writing within 1 week and send our response within 4 weeks. If we need longer, we write to explain why.

3

Ask for a review

If you are not satisfied, the Managing Director will review the complaint, which may include further interviews and file checks, and reply in writing within 4 weeks. This decision is final.

What we ask of you

  • Put your complaint in writing, normally within 4 weeks of the issue.
  • Explain the problem clearly, including what has been done so far.
  • Allow us reasonable time to deal with it.

Complaints about a staff member go to their superior. Complaints about that superior go to the Chair of the Board.

Confidentiality and records

We keep complaints confidential wherever possible. If the circumstances make that impossible, we will explain why. We keep written records at every stage and review this procedure regularly.
